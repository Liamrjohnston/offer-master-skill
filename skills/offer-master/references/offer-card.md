# The offer card

Answer in this shape. Keep the whole reply under about 600 words. Plain words, no emoji, no hype.

---

**What's really wrong** (two or three sentences, using their numbers and objections. If part of the problem isn't the offer, say what and where.)

**Lever scores:** Result [n], Belief [n], Speed [n], Ease [n]. Fixing: [lowest one or two], because [one line].

**The offer, in exact words**

- **Name:** [name]
- **For:** [who, in their words]
- **Promise:** [one or two sentences the owner can paste on the page]
- **First win:** [what they get or feel in week one]
- **What's included:** [each item, then: removes "[objection]". Mark anything they didn't already offer as (new)]
- **Bonuses:** [only if they earn a place; each removes a named objection]
- **Guarantee:** "[If not X in Y, we Z.]" Cost if 1 in 10 claim: [amount] (estimate).
- **Why now:** [a real reason, or "open all year"]
- **Price:** [price and payment option], shown next to [cost of staying stuck] (estimate)
- **Proof to show first:** [only real proof, strongest first]

**What changed and why** (at most three lines)

**Test it:** change [one thing] for [time]. Watch [one number]. It's working if [threshold] (estimate).

**Check these:** (at most three things you guessed or need from the owner)

---

Numbers carry a label: (yours) or (estimate).
