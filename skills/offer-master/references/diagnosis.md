# Diagnosis: where buyers drop off and why

## Walk the path

Write the owner's path as stages with their own numbers, then work out each step's rate yourself and label it (estimate).

Example: 400 page views, 3 calls booked, 1 sale. Views to calls is about 0.75 percent (estimate). Calls to sales is about 33 percent (estimate). The leak is before the call, so the page and the offer on it come first.

Compare each stage with the owner's own past numbers if they have them. Don't quote industry averages as facts. If you give a rough benchmark, call it a rough guide, not a statistic.

| Where it drops | Most likely cause | What to fix first |
|---|---|---|
| Hardly anyone sees it | Traffic | Reach and content. Say plainly that the offer is not the main problem |
| Many see it, few enquire | The promise, proof or risk on the page | Promise, proof, guarantee |
| Many enquire, few buy | Price shock, unclear scope, weak follow up, risk | How the price is shown, scope, guarantee, follow up |
| They buy, then ask for refunds or quit | Delivery or expectations | The first win and what's included, before more selling |
| They buy once and never again | Nothing to continue into | A next step after the first result |

## What buyers say and what it usually means

| What they say | The belief behind it | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| "I'll think about it" | "I'm not sure this works for someone like me" or "If it fails, I lose" | Belief, proof, guarantee |
| "Too expensive" | "I don't see why it's worth this much" | Result, cost of staying stuck, payment plan |
| "I can do it myself" / "A free app does this" | "I'm buying information, and information is free" | Show what they get that free can't give: the doing, the checking, the fixing |
| "What exactly do I get?" | "I can't picture it, so I can't judge it" | A fixed scope, named deliverables, a timeline |
| "How do I know it'll work?" | "No proof I'll get my result" | Proof, a measurable promise, guarantee |
| "Not right now" | "Nothing changes if I wait" | Cost of waiting, a real reason to start now |
| "My nephew / a friend can do it" | "Cheaper option looks the same to me" | Show the difference in outcome, not features |
| They go quiet after the quote | Price shock, or the quote doesn't remind them why | How the quote is presented and followed up |

Some objections are fair. If people keep saying the same thing and they're right, change the offer.
