# A full run, from brief to card

This business is made up. It is here so you can see the shape of a finished run. Do not copy its numbers, name, proof or price onto a real offer. A real offer uses only that owner's facts.

## The brief

"My offer isn't selling. I do monthly bookkeeping for electricians and plumbers with one to five staff. $350 a month. About 80 people see the page, 12 enquire, 2 become clients. They say 'my accountant already does this' or 'I'll sort it at tax time'. I have 14 clients. One, Dave, an electrician, was six weeks behind on invoices. He now invoices every Friday, and what customers owed him dropped from $40,000 to $11,000 in one quarter. I can take 20 clients. No countdown timers."

## How the stages land

**Diagnosis.** 80 see it, 12 enquire, 2 buy (all his). The steep drop is enquire to buy, so the leak is the offer, not traffic. "My accountant already does this" means he thinks this is tax prep. "I'll sort it at tax time" means waiting feels free. Current offer in one line: monthly bookkeeping for small trade businesses, $350, and the page never says what is different from the accountant.

**Levers.** Result 6, Belief 4, Speed 3, Ease 6. Fix Belief and Speed. Those are the two sentences buyers actually say.

**Included.** Proof to lead with, in his words: 14 clients, and Dave's invoices. Promise stays inside that proof. No "get paid faster" as a guarantee of income. First win is the first Friday invoice run, in week one, because that is what Dave's result was built on. Cut "full-service accounting" if the page says it. He does not do tax returns. One bonus: a one-page list of what to photograph on a job so the invoice can go out the same week. It kills "I don't have time to gather the paperwork." No dollar value on it.

**Guarantee.** Main fear is paying for something the accountant already covers. One capped guarantee: do the first month, and if the Friday invoice run is not in place, month one is refunded. Two conditions: he sends the job photos within two days, and he shows up to the weekly 20-minute call. Cost if 1 in 10 claim: about $35 of the $350 (estimate). He can afford that. Not open-ended free bookkeeping.

**Why now, name, price.** He did not give a deadline or a count of open places. Why now: Open all year. Places: [N] open, because "I can take 20" is a ceiling, not spots left. Cost of waiting: invoices that sit for weeks are cash he has already earned and not collected. Name: The Friday Invoice Retainer. A plumber could text that. Price stays $350 a month (his). Payment option: none needed at this price. No "worth $X" and no crossed-out price.

**Honesty check.** Dave's result stays Dave's, past tense, one client. The drop from $40,000 to $11,000 is not rewritten as "clients collect $29,000". The 10 to 40 percent forecast is not on the card. The bonus is marked (new).

## The card

**What's really wrong:** 80 people see the page and 12 enquire (yours), but 2 buy (yours). They are not confused about bookkeeping. They think their accountant already covers it, or that tax time is soon enough. The page never shows the weekly invoice habit, which is the part the accountant is not doing.

**Lever scores:** Result 6, Belief 4, Speed 3, Ease 6. **Fixing:** Belief and Speed, because "my accountant already does this" and "I'll sort it at tax time" are the lines they use.

### The offer

- **Name:** The Friday Invoice Retainer
- **For:** Electricians and plumbers with a small crew who are behind on invoices
- **Promise:** "Your invoices go out every Friday, and you can see who still owes you, without waiting for tax time."
- **First win:** The first Friday invoice run done in week one, on jobs you already finished.
- **What's included:**
  - Monthly bookkeeping: removes "my accountant already does the books"
  - Weekly 20-minute call: removes "I won't stay on top of it"
  - Friday invoice checklist (new): removes "I don't have the paperwork ready"
- **Cut:** Anything that calls this tax prep or full-service accounting. That is why they say the accountant already does it.
- **Bonuses:** One (new): a one-page photo list for job paperwork, so Friday is possible. Kills "I don't have time to gather it." No value claim.
- **Guarantee:** "Do month one. If the Friday invoice run is not in place, month one is refunded." Conditions: job photos in within two days, and you attend the weekly call. Cap: one month refunded. Cost if 1 in 10 claim: about $35 per sale (estimate).
- **Why now:** Open all year. [N] places open. Cost of waiting: invoices that sit are cash already earned and not collected (estimate, from Dave's pattern, not a promise).
- **Price:** $350 a month (yours). Shown next to the cost of staying weeks behind, not next to a made-up anchor.
- **Proof to show first:** 14 clients (yours). Dave, an electrician, was six weeks behind. He now invoices every Friday, and what customers owed him dropped from $40,000 to $11,000 in one quarter (yours, one client).

**Honesty check:** Dave's numbers stay his. No income promise, no spots left, no bonus value.

**Test this month:** Add the Friday line and Dave's result to the enquiry reply for the next 12 enquiries. Watch enquiries to clients. It's working if 4 of those 12 become clients (estimate).
