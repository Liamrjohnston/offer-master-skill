# Why now, the name and the price

## Why now

Only use a reason that is real, the buyer could check, and the owner will stick to.

Real reasons:
- **Capacity:** "I take [N] clients at a time. [N] places are open." Only if the owner updates it honestly. Knowing the maximum doesn't tell you how many are free: leave open places as [N] unless the owner said.
- **A real start date:** a group that starts on a set day.
- **A real price change:** the price goes up on a date, and it really does.
- **A seasonal reason:** a deadline in the buyer's world (tax season, summer, a launch date).
- **The cost of waiting:** what another month of the problem costs them. Always available, always honest.

If none of these is true, the honest answer is "open all year". Say so. Never invent spots, timers, or "price goes up soon".

## The name

- Test: could the buyer text it to a friend and the friend would get what it is? If not, rename it.
- Say who it's for or what it does. Plain beats clever.
- Never put a money, weight or health result with a time frame in the name ("Lose 10 kg in 12 Weeks", "$10k Month Method"). Results go in proof, described as what past clients got.

## The price

- Show it next to the cost of staying stuck, in the buyer's terms (estimate).
- Match the billing to the promise. A 12 week program reads better as a 12 week price than an open monthly fee.
- Offer a payment plan before any discount. If the plan costs more in total, say the total plainly. Never sell a costlier plan as a saving.
- Never discount to win a new customer. It cheapens the offer and draws buyers who leave first. Rewards for existing customers are fine.
- A higher price only works if Result and Belief go up with it. Don't raise the price just to look premium.
