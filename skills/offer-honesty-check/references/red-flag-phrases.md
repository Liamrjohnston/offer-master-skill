# Red flag phrases

Words that usually mean a claim needs checking, and honest swaps. A phrase on this list isn't automatically wrong; it means "trace this one". `scripts/flag_claims.py` scans for the same groups.

## Fake value and anchors

| Phrase | Problem | Honest swap |
|---|---|---|
| "worth $X", "valued at", "a $X value" | Value nobody paid | Say what the item does |
| "total value", "over $X in bonuses" | Sum of invented values | List the bonuses and the doubt each removes |
| "regular price", "normally", "was $X", crossed out prices | Price never charged | The real price, next to the cost of the problem |
| "you save", "X% off" | Saving against a made up number | A payment option, or less for less |

## Fake scarcity and pressure

| Phrase | Problem | Honest swap |
|---|---|---|
| "only N spots left", "spots left", "almost full" | Count the owner didn't give | "[N] places open" |
| "ends tonight", "last chance", "today only" | Rolling deadline | A real date, once, or nothing |
| "limited time", "price going up soon" | No date behind it | The real date and new price, or nothing |
| "countdown", "timer", "hurry" | Pressure without a reason | The cost of waiting |
| "N people are viewing this" | Made up | Real proof |

## Promises nobody can keep

| Phrase | Problem | Honest swap |
|---|---|---|
| "guaranteed results", "guaranteed to" | A result outside the owner's control | The capped guarantee on what they control |
| "lose N kg", "drop N sizes" in a time frame | Health result with a time frame | What the program does; past results as proof |
| "double", "triple", "10x", "N times more" | Usually invented | The owner's real before and after |
| "make $X", "earn $X a month" | Income promise | What past clients got, with context |
| "risk free", "no risk" | Rarely literally true | "Backed by [the guarantee]" |
| "never", "always", "every time", "100%" | Absolute claim | Say what usually happens, honestly |

## Borrowed authority and vague proof

| Phrase | Problem | Honest swap |
|---|---|---|
| "studies show", "research proves", "experts agree" | No source | Cut, or cite the owner's source |
| "industry average", "on average", "typically" | Whose average? | The owner's own numbers |
| "most clients", "most people", "N% of clients" | Needs counted evidence | "[n] of [total] clients", from the owner |
| "proven system", "proven method" | Proven by whom? | The proof itself |
| "thousands of happy customers", "N+ clients" | Rounded up | The exact count the owner gave |
| "as seen in", "featured in", "award winning" | Needs a real mention | Cut unless the owner names it |

## Hype words (not dishonest, just weak)

"Ultimate", "secret", "revolutionary", "game changing", "life changing", "unlock", "skyrocket", "supercharge", "insane", "no brainer". Replace with the concrete thing.
