# Before and after (fictional)

A tutoring business: online maths tutoring for secondary school students, $60 an hour, booked one session at a time. 20 enquiries a month, 4 become regular students. Parents say "we'll book when the exams get closer". Proof: 25 students tutored; one student went from a D to a B over a school year; a parent wrote "he actually asks for help now". Limit: 18 hours a week.

## A weak card

> Your offer lacks a compelling value proposition! Here's a rebuilt, irresistible offer.
>
> **Name:** Maths Mastery Accelerator
> **Promise:** Guaranteed grade jump in 8 weeks or your money back!
> **Bonuses:** Exam Cheat Sheet Pack (worth $199), Parent Progress Portal (worth $299). Total value: $1,438!
> **Price:** ~~$960~~ just $480!
> **Urgency:** Only 3 spots left!
> **Tests to run:** A/B test the headline, test price points, test the guarantee, run ads, add a quiz funnel.

What's wrong with it: hype name with no meaning to a friend; a grade promised with a time frame; invented bonus values and total; a crossed out price never charged; a spot count nobody gave; five tests, none measurable; no diagnosis using the owner's numbers.

## A strong card (shortened)

> **What's really wrong:** 20 parents ask each month (yours) and 4 start (yours). "We'll book when the exams get closer" means they see tutoring as a last minute fix, and paying by the hour lets them wait. The offer sells hours, not a plan.
>
> **Lever scores:** Result 5, Belief 6, Speed 4, Ease 6. **Fixing:** Result and Speed, because parents can't see what steady tutoring gets that a few cram sessions don't.
>
> - **Name:** Term Maths Plan
> - **For:** Parents of secondary students who are falling behind in maths
> - **Promise:** "A weekly plan built from your child's own gaps, so the topics are solid before the exam, not crammed the week before."
> - **First win:** A gap report after the first session, showing exactly which topics to fix first.
> - **What's included:**
>   - Weekly 1 hour session: removes "we'll wait"
>   - Gap report after session one (new): removes "will this help my child?"
>   - Short monthly note to parents (new): removes "I can't tell if it's working"
> - **Cut:** Single session booking for new students; keep it for exam week top ups.
> - **Bonuses:** None.
> - **Guarantee:** "If the gap report doesn't show you clearly what we'll work on, the first session is free." Cap: one session. Cost if 1 in 10 claim: 1 session at $60 per 10 new students (estimate).
> - **Why now:** "I have 18 teaching hours a week (yours). [N] are open for this term." Cost of waiting: each week left until the exams is one fewer week to fix gaps properly.
> - **Price:** $60 a session (yours), billed monthly for the term.
> - **Proof to show first:** "He actually asks for help now." One student went from a D to a B over a school year. 25 students tutored.
>
> **Test this month:** Offer the Term Plan to the next 20 enquiries. Watch how many start. It's working if 6 or more start (estimate).
>
> **Check these:** Are you happy to bill monthly for the term? How many of your 18 hours are free?

What makes it strong: the diagnosis uses the owner's numbers and the parents' words; the promise is what the tutor controls; the result appears only as proof, exactly as given; every new item is marked; the guarantee is small, capped and costed; capacity is shown honestly; one measurable test.
