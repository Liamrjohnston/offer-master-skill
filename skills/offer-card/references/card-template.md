# Card template, field by field

## Length budget (about 750 words at most)

| Part | Words |
|---|---|
| What's really wrong | 40 to 70 |
| Lever scores and fixing | 20 to 35 |
| The offer (all fields) | 250 to 350 |
| Page section, with the numbers line | 120 to 200 |
| Honesty check | 10 to 25 |
| Test this month | 25 to 45 |
| Check these | 25 to 60 |

If it runs long, cut in this order: explanations inside fields, a third bonus, a third included item's detail, the cost of waiting wording. Never cut the guarantee cap, the labels, the test or "Check these".

## Fields

**What's really wrong.** Lead with the leak, in their numbers: "400 people see the page (yours), about 1 buys (yours)." Then what the objections mean. Then, if relevant, what isn't the offer: "At 6 enquiries a month (yours), even a strong offer needs more people asking; that's a traffic job." No industry benchmarks.

**Lever scores.** Four integers. Name the one or two you're fixing and the objection that points there. Don't explain every score.

**Name.** Two to five words, passes the text test, no result plus time claim.

**For.** Who, in the buyer's words, and matching where the proof came from. If the proof is from one kind of client, "For" names that kind first.

**Promise.** One or two sentences in quotes, ready to paste. Specific, wanted, inside the proof, answers the top objection. Promises the work or the controllable outcome; results appear as proof.

**First win.** Concrete and in week one. Something the buyer can see or feel, not "onboarding".

**What's included.** Three to six items. Each: "[item]: removes '[doubt]'". Items the owner doesn't offer yet get (new).

**Cut.** One line. What to drop, merge or stop saying. If nothing, "Nothing to cut."

**Bonuses.** "None" is fine. At most three here. Each (new) and tied to a named doubt. Never a dollar value.

**Guarantee.** The exact words in quotes, then the cap, then the cost line: "Cost if 1 in 10 claim: 6 hours at $60 = $360 per 10 sales (estimate)." If an input is missing: "[hours] × [your rate]".

**Why now.** The honest reason, or "Open all year". Then the cost of waiting, one line, labelled.

**Price.** The exact price they wrote is (yours). A split or a multiplied total is (estimate) on this line and on the numbers line. $199 a month is not "$597 (yours)". $3,500 is not "$1,750 (yours)". No crossed out price, no "worth", no "you save".

**Proof to show first.** Exactly as given. Names only if the owner gave them.

## The page section

Written for the buyer, not the owner. It should read like a real page.

- Headline: the promise.
- Two to four short lines: who it's for, the first win, the strongest proof.
- The included list, short.
- The guarantee in one sentence.
- Price and payment option, then one call to action ("Book a call", "Reply 'start'").
- Only the owner's numbers. A worked out figure (a cost of waiting) can appear only if the inputs are the owner's; otherwise leave it out of the page.
- No labels inside it. No em dash in the copy. Two sentences, not a dash. Then one line: "Numbers on this page: $1,200 (yours), $600 (estimate), 14 cafés (yours)." A cap or a deadline you chose is (estimate), not (yours).

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| An essay before the card | Delete it. Start with "What's really wrong" |
| "(yours)" and "(estimate)" scattered through the page copy | Clean copy, one numbers line under it |
| A bonus "worth $97" | Remove the value |
| "Only 2 places left" from a capacity of 2 a month | "[N] places open" |
| Five tests | One test |
| Seven "Check these" | The three that matter most |
| Proof relabelled ("7 clinic sites" from "7 sites") | Exactly as given |
| A promise of results by a date | Promise the work; results as proof |
| Assumed inputs in sums ($50 an hour from nowhere) | A [placeholder] and a "Check these" question |
| A multiplied total or a split marked (yours) | (estimate). $199 a month for 3 months is $597 (estimate) |
| A check-in call rewritten as "30 minutes" or "video form check" | Keep their words. A new method is (new) |
