# Naming

## The test

Could the buyer text the name to a friend, with no other context, and the friend would know what it is and roughly who it's for? If not, rename it.

## Ingredients

Pick two or three:

- **Who it's for:** new managers, café owners, first time landlords.
- **What it does or gets them:** books done, garden reset, first app shipped.
- **The time or format:** 6 week, weekend, one day, monthly.
- **A container word:** plan, program, setup, sprint, clinic, course, kit, review.

## Patterns (examples are fictional)

| Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|
| Plain description | Monthly Bookkeeping, Puppy Basics Course |
| Who plus what | New Manager Coaching, Café Books Done For You |
| Result plus container | Garden Reset, First App Sprint |
| Time plus container | 6 Week Conversation Course, One Day Website Fix |
| What it replaces | Spreadsheet Free Books, No More Phone Tag Booking Setup |

## How to pick

Draft five from different patterns. Score each:

1. A friend would get it from the name alone.
2. It says who or what, not a mood.
3. Two to five words, easy to say out loud.
4. No result plus time claim, no hype word.
5. Not already a well known product or trademarked method.

Pick the one that passes all five. If two pass, pick the plainer one.

## Avoid

| Avoid | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A money, weight or health result with a time frame | "Lose 10 kg in 12 Weeks", "$10k Month Method" | A promise nobody can guarantee, and ad platforms and regulators dislike it |
| Clever puns | "Thyme to Grow" | The friend has to ask what it is |
| Hype words | "Ultimate", "Mastery", "Secret", "Blueprint" | Reads as a sales trick |
| Jargon | "Omnichannel Growth Accelerator" | Seller's words, not the buyer's |
| Acronyms | "The P.A.C.E. System" | Means nothing to a buyer |
| Borrowed framework names | Well known trademarked method names | Not the owner's to use, and it misleads |

Results belong in proof ("Ana filled 11 Tuesday slots"), not in the name.
