# Urgency that's real

Urgency works because buyers put things off. Fake urgency works once, then the buyer notices, and the trust is gone for good. Real urgency works every time because it's true.

## The honest reasons

| Reason | When it's true | How to keep it honest |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity | The owner can only serve so many at once | Show open places as [N] and update them. A maximum is not the number free |
| A start date | Groups or cohorts start on set days | Publish the date. Late joiners wait for the next one |
| A price change | The owner has decided to raise the price on a date | Announce the date and do it. Don't extend it |
| A season | The buyer's world has a deadline (tax, weather, school year, a launch) | Name the real deadline and how long the work takes |
| A bonus that ends | A real extra offered to early buyers | End it on the date. Don't bring it back next week |
| The cost of waiting | Always | Say what another month of the problem costs, from real inputs |

## The six questions

Before using any reason, all six must be yes:

1. Is it true right now?
2. Will the owner enforce it every time?
3. Could the buyer check it?
4. Will it be honest the next time it's used, not repeated every week?
5. Can the owner answer "is this really limited?" truthfully, face to face?
6. Is it near the point of decision (the price, the button), not hidden?

## When urgency isn't needed

- Subscriptions people can start any time.
- Small impulse buys.
- Repeat buyers who already trust the owner.
- Offers where the buyer's own deadline does the work (a wedding, a tax date).

In those cases, "Open all year" plus the cost of waiting is the right answer.

## Refuse these, and offer the honest version

| Tactic | Why it's wrong | Honest version |
|---|---|---|
| A countdown timer that resets | It's a lie the buyer can catch by reloading | A real date, or none |
| "Only 3 spots left" that never changes | Invented scarcity | "[N] places open", updated |
| "Price goes up soon" with no date | Pressure with nothing behind it | A real date and price, or nothing |
| "Ends tonight" every night | Fake deadline | A bonus that really ends, once |
| "12 people are viewing this" | Made up | Nothing; real proof does more |
| A bonus held back for the next "last chance" | Manipulation | Put the bonus in the offer or leave it out |
| Calling a capacity limit "only 2 left" | The owner didn't say how many are free | "[N] places open" |

## Wording that stays honest

- "I take [max] clients at a time, so I can [the reason it matters to them]. [N] places are open."
- "The next group starts [date]."
- "From [date], the price is [new price]. Anyone who starts before then pays [current price]."
- "This is open all year. The only cost of waiting is [the problem] for another month."
